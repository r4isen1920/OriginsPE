# Release v6.0.0

Adds 2 new Origins (Child of Zeus, Vampire), 1 new Class (Athlete), a full codebase rewrite for maintainability, plus balance, bug fix, QoL, and UI updates.

# New Origins

## Child of Zeus

Thunder-born descendants with strong sky and lightning powers.

- [Wrath of Olympus]: Summon and throw a Trident that explodes on impact, damaging mobs and players. 30s cooldown.
- [Divine Ascent]: Double jump and hover in clouds. Immune to fall damage.
- [Static Energy]: Applies Slowness for 6s to mobs and players you hit, or that hit you.
- [Discharge]: Hitting a Slowed target has a 30% chance to stun for 1-2s. Stunned enemies take +3 health damage per 2 unique metallic items in your inventory.
- [Surge Protection]: Immune to lightning damage.
- [Claustrophobic]: Gain Weakness underground or outside the Overworld.
- [Severed Connection]: Wrath of Olympus only works in the Overworld.

## Vampire

Blood-based hunters with mobility, execution, and sustain tools.

- [Bat Form]: Transform into a bat to fly. In bat form you do not burn in daylight, but cannot attack or use tools. Use again to revert.
- [Bloodthirsty]: Cannot eat normal food; must drink blood. Blood restores when hitting living entities. At 0 blood, gain Weakness and take continuous damage.
- [Death Sense]: Targets at 20% health or lower are executed (fatal hit) when damaged.
- [Nocturnal]: Gain Night Vision and Speed at night.
- [Regenerative]: Faster healing while injured, but drains blood quickly. Cannot regenerate while burning.
- [Undead]: Immune to poison and freezing; weak to fire and sunlight; can breathe underwater.

# New Classes

## Athlete

High-mobility class focused on stamina and agility.

- [Speedy]: Faster walk, swim, and sprint speed.
- [Strong]: Punches deal +1 damage.

# Changes and Fixes

- Re-wrote the entire Add-On foundation for better maintainability and performance.
- Reworked the cooldown system to be more consistent and responsive.
- Various UI polish across the Origin picker and Options screens.
- Numerous balancing tweaks and bug fixes across existing Origins and Classes.
