# Release v6.0.0

This update brings brand new Origins: the Child of Zeus, and the Vampire! Additionally, one new Class has been added: the Athlete. The architecture and foundation of the Add-On's code has also been re-written from the ground up to allow for maintainability and readability, alongside many balancing tweaks, bug fixes, QoL and UI changes.

## New Origin: Child of Zeus

Descendants of the great thunder God, capable of controlling the lightning itself.

- [Wrath of Olympus]: Conjures a powerful throwable Trident on your hand, creating an explosion and damaging mobs and players on impact. Has a 30-second cooldown.
- [Divine Ascent]: Gifted with the power of skies, you can double jump able to hover and float around under a foam of clouds. You are also immune from fall damage.
- [Static Energy]: The power of electricity gushes in you. Applies slowness on mobs & players you hit, or hit you, which lasts for 6 seconds.
- [Discharge]: Whenever you deal damage on other mobs & players that are slowed, there is a 30% chance you will stun them for 1-2 seconds. Stunned enemies additionally take 3 health of damage for every 2 unique metallic items in your inventory.
- [Surge Protection]: You are immune from lightning damage.
- [Claustrophobic]: As a God from the skies, you aren't used to being underneath. You gain weakness whenever you're underground or not in the Overworld.
- [Severed Connection]: The End and the Nether have no skies. You are unable to call upon the Wrath of Olympus on dimensions other than the Overworld.

## New Origin: Vampire

Creatures of the night that trade the warmth of the sun for power drawn from blood.

- [Bloodthirst]: Striking mobs and players drains their life to heal you, but you must keep feeding or slowly waste away.
- [Nocturnal]: You grow stronger under the moon and suffer weakness in direct sunlight.

## New Class: Athlete

Peak physical specimens who rely on raw stamina and agility over brute force.

- [Second Wind]: Sprinting long enough rewards you with a burst of speed and regeneration.
- [Sure Footing]: Short drops no longer hurt, and you scale ladders and vines faster.

## Changes and Fixes

- Re-wrote the entire Add-On foundation for better maintainability and performance.
- Reworked the cooldown system to be more consistent and responsive.
- Various UI polish across the Origin picker and Options screens.
- Numerous balancing tweaks and bug fixes across existing Origins and Classes.
